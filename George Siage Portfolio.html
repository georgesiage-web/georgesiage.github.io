About

Media with a purpose

I’ve always seen media as more than just cameras, editing, or technology; for me, it’s a way of sharing the love of Jesus with people.

Through my experience in livestreaming, content creation, and digital communications, I’ve had the privilege of using media to support the work of the gospel. What makes this work meaningful to me is knowing that a message shared through a screen can reach someone who might never walk through the doors of a church.

My heart is to keep learning, growing, and using the gifts God has given me to tell others about Jesus, encourage the Church, and help take the everlasting gospel to the world.

Working together

Most of my work comes through contracts, so I build each project around your team, your goals and your timeline.

I join your team

I can lead a production as producer or slot into a role you need filled, whether that is shooting, editing, sound or script. I share drafts early and welcome notes at every stage.

I adapt to your setup

Different editing software, different cameras, different workflows. I learn what your project runs on and work inside it, so you never have to change your pipeline for me.

I start from your message

Every project begins with what you want people to understand and feel. The script, the shots and the sound all follow from that.
